Our History

DOMINANCE OF
GILLIES

Chepauk Super Gillies is the most successful team in the history of the Tamil Nadu Premier League (TNPL), proudly representing the vibrant city of Chennai.

Owned by Metronation Chennai Television Pvt. Ltd., a Daily Thanthi Group company, the Super Gillies are known for their championship spirit, consistency, and deep rooted commitment to Tamil Nadu cricket. With four championship titles and five final appearances, we have set the benchmark for excellence and consistency in the league.

Join us as we continue our legacy of excellence, proudly waving the Chennai flag high in every match we play.
